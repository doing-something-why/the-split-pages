Common Questions

How do I split a bill?

Take a photo of your receipt, or use voice/text to describe what everyone ordered. The app uses AI to extract items and assign them to people automatically.

How do I manage my subscription?

Go to Settings in the app, then tap "Manage Subscription." You can also manage it through your iPhone's Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Reset All Data." All dinner history, friend names, and settings will be permanently deleted.

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device. Voice, photo, and text data is processed by OpenAI's API via encrypted connection but is not stored on any server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

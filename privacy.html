Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 26, 2026

The Split ("we", "our", or "the app") is a bill-splitting app that helps you divide restaurant bills among friends. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Data We Collect

We do not create user accounts. The app works entirely without sign-up or login.

  • Dinner data (item names, people names, prices, notes) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. It never leaves your device except when processed by AI services as described below.
  • Voice recordings are temporarily recorded on your device and sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription. Audio is deleted from your device immediately after transcription.
  • Bill photos are stored locally and sent to OpenAI's API (with Google Gemini as automatic fallback if OpenAI is unavailable) for item extraction. Photos are not stored on any server.
  • Text notes are sent to OpenAI's API (with Google Gemini as automatic fallback) for parsing into structured data.
  • The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifying information. Voice, photo, and text content sent to AI providers contains only ad-hoc bill data (item names, friend nicknames, prices) — no name, email, phone, payment details, or other PII.
  • Purchase information is handled by Apple and RevenueCat for subscription management. We do not store your payment details.

Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI — processes voice (Whisper), photos (Vision), and text (GPT) to extract bill items and match them to people. Data is sent via encrypted HTTPS. OpenAI's privacy policy: openai.com/privacy
  • Google Gemini — used as automatic fallback for photo and text parsing if the OpenAI request fails. Data is sent via encrypted HTTPS. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and purchase verification. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple — handles in-app purchases and App Store transactions.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not share or sell any data to third parties
  • We do not use cookies

Data Retention

All dinner data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time through the app's Settings. Voice recordings are deleted immediately after transcription. We do not retain any data on our servers.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
